# Additional analyses of the frozen primary hardware campaign

Run `python analysis/build_additional_analyses.py` with NumPy and OpenCV available. This script reads all 24 primary JSON/JSONL pairs and the retained September calibration correspondence record. It imports no robot code and changes no trial data. Generated artifacts are `additional_analyses.json`, five `additional_*_table.tex` files and `additional_analyses_manifest.json`. The tables require `booktabs` and `float` (`[H]`).

All analyses are retrospective. They describe the same 24 runs and add no independent replication. The source attachment's proposed numbers were hypotheses, not inputs substituted into results.

## What was confirmed or corrected

- **Calibration:** the actual deployed estimator, OpenCV `findHomography(method=0)` with float32 fitting coordinates, gives retained-eight-point in-sample mean/max **0.692/1.190 mm** and leave-one-out mean/max **1.819/2.929 mm**. The proposed 1.95/3.26 mm is not reproduced with this estimator. The ninth point's omitted world coordinate and old residual are recorded, but its pixel coordinate is absent; it is not invented or restored. Cross-validation remains conditional on a data-selected eight-point set, not independent camera accuracy or a clearance uncertainty bound.
- **Decision occupancy:** consecutive decision intervals are assigned to the earlier decision's final logged reason, exactly once. At slow cadence the score-hold occupancy averages **22.837 s** for AEGIS. The complete carry-to-delivery mean is **54.368 s**; an average **8.125 s** from final decision to delivery remains unassigned because it includes final command execution, checks and placement. These values do not measure physical standstill, reaction time, or braking time.
- **Geometry repeatability:** use the earliest eligible row for each unique hazard frame, with an OBJECT verdict, current non-destination-guard geometry and no unmatched depth-only fallback. A componentwise median per run is followed by an across-run sample SD. This gives **3.521 mm in x**, **7.745 mm in y**, and representative radii **35.799–38.177 mm**. The broad alternative including unmatched depth has one 30 mm median; that is not evidence that the box physically shrank. This repeatability of internal estimates does not eliminate operator-placement confounding or validate physical separation.
- **Chronology:** header clocks place first-to-last run starts at **15:36:35–16:08:34 UTC** (31.985 min), or **16:36:35–17:08:34 Europe/London**. The final delivery occurs at **16:09:35 UTC**, making first start to final delivery **33.000 min**, not 32 min. The normal methods were ordered AEGIS, Trust12, Trust32; slow methods reversed that order; each direction pair and both repetitions follow that pattern. This is not randomization or proof of constant conditions.
- **Nominal block signs:** matching rate, destination and filename repetition gives eight nominal blocks. AEGIS has larger minimum command-estimated separation in 8/8 against Trust12 and 7/8 against Trust32. Exact two-sided binomial sign reference values are 0.0078125 and 0.0703125. Their independent/equiprobable-sign assumptions are not established in the nonrandomized sequence; no calibrated significance or causal advantage is claimed.

## Integrity and interpretation

The script retains all 24 primary runs, verifies unchanged hashes for all 48 input logs, reproduces the existing normal/slow carry-to-delivery means and reconciles decision intervals plus unassigned endpoints to each duration. Each record in JSON includes the interval classification, count and source information for independent inspection.

The chronological table displays UTC explicitly rather than silently assuming a timezone from file timestamps. No filesystem modification times are used. Stopped decisions may be followed by continued physical motion or blocking commands. Logged geometric medians combine perception and tracker behavior, and should not be called externally measured placement precision.
